The population model

  1. 1 After reading the 705 community records (A1)

    The aggregator wrote a note on each community, tracing what its members did and what the evidence left unclear. Ask what stood out about r/PlaceNL before the aggregator built its coding scheme. Read the A1 record.

  2. 2 After the coding scheme was fixed (B4)

    The aggregator has settled the readers' disagreements and frozen the scheme of themes. Ask why a theme sits where it does. Its record.

  3. 3 After the first population model (C5)

    The aggregator has written the integrated account, with its table, model and figure. Ask how the dimensions connect and where the account is weakest. Its record.

  4. 4 After the final population model (D3)

    Every community coder has tested the model against its case, and the aggregator has revised it where the evidence supported a change. Ask what the return changed and what it refused. Its record.

  5. 5 After the organizational forms were carved (D4)

    The aggregator has grouped the communities that asked for similar changes, which gave the six candidate forms. Ask why a community falls in one form and not another. Its record.
