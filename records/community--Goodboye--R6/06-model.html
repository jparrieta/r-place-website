Dynamics, projection and what would break

No loop is claimed: model/loops.jsonl is intentionally empty. Hour 73 makes further placement on this canvas unavailable; memory, affiliation and choosing new work remain possible. The same issue reopened in a later thread is a sequence unless a linked mechanism supplies feedback.

Projecting retained arrows gives D01 to D02 through A03 and internal D02 relations through A01 and A02. The cross-dimension arrow is a specific response to a restoration offer, not a general dimension-level causal law. Negotiation, specification and reversion constraints do not abstract into one universal "control" mechanism. Report the pair as structural arrow mass, not as an additional dimension arrow.

For D01, incoming retained mass is empty, outgoing is A03, internal is empty. For D02, incoming is A03, outgoing is empty, internal is A01 and A02. For D03, each retained mass is empty. The model should break where an exchange supplies no explicit uptake yet the account asserts revision; where a claimed personal concession is treated as binding; or where intended specifications are taken to control independently operated scripts. These are mechanism-level failure conditions, not generic corpus-size limits. If a retained tally and implemented continuation appeared, the purpose account would change.
