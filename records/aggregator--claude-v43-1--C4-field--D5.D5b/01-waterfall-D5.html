The waterfall: one walk from the account to the quotes and back

Stage D5, leg D5b, 2026-10-26 UTC. The model is the one frozen at D3 (46384686…), the model every branch hangs from. The return was served at pass 15 (e15f9647…b3fecd). The two held proposals (QD4-001) are stated in full at the end of this file.

The walk goes down six steps: the account, one branch, one subform, one room's model, the room's adaptation lines and the room's quotes. At each step I say what the level adds and whether it contradicts the level above. Then I walk back up and say what each lower level does to the level above it. Last, I read the walk against prior.md's expectation of what the lower levels describe. The canvas comes after the walk, in its own section, and changes none of it (rule 7).

Down

1. The account (account.md, pass 15)

The account states the close in one paragraph. Placement stopped at hour 73. Before it, rooms celebrate and record the work, and they carry "the record on into banners, later canvases and groups announced to carry on in 113 of 145" rooms [A09, P13] (F-004). After hour 73, rooms curate the record. The account keeps the two acts apart: "Curating the record is a different act from carrying the work on into later groups [P13]."

What this level gives is a population pattern in counts. A09 is an arrow from recording (P12) to carrying on (P13). Its count is an order on the clock across rooms. It is not a cause shown inside each room. P13's own words, as stage D4 gives them, say that near the close and after it the room decides what of its effort to keep.

2. The branch: F11, carrying on as renewed work, begun before the close (43 members)

F11 shares one key: an add-step line on P13 at the fourth-order theme Transition (T4-028), one line per member. The branch adds that in these rooms carrying on is new work, not a choice about work already done. Members chose a venue, called others, posted coordinates, built, and lost or defended the work again. In 20 members it is building again on another canvas, in 10 a remake in other media, and in 7 the mark adopted into the room.

Does it contradict the account? In part. The account counts A09 as an order: the record, then carrying on. The branch's lines restrict A09 in 15 members, on the ground that no record is shown to have caused the carrying on (F11's section 7). Both can hold, since an order across rooms is not a cause inside one. But the branch gives no support for reading A09 as a mechanism. The branch's name also says more than its members show. By the first hour each key line cites, 11 of 43 members begin before the close and 32 after. F11's account proposes a renaming for that reason.

Does it contradict F11? No; it is one of F11's kinds. Does it contradict the account? No; it repeats the account's spine at a second site. One misfit is already on record. The atlas is not a measure of skill or of what the room intended, and it has no measure of whether the room went on.
