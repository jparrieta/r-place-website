Claim and puzzle

Participants organized through repeated calls, specifications and negotiated commitments, while control over placers and space remained divided. Their work had a recognizable object without a settled specification. The strongest local connections concern speakers' stated reasons for appeals and withdrawal. The public record supports a bounded process account; it does not establish that a unified Czech organization directed every participant or that pacts kept every artwork alive.

The puzzle is how help and joint pictures remain possible while participants cannot bind their own crowd or trust a neighbour's every member. P01 and P02 distinguish assembling contributions from settling a design. K01 preserves the inability to redirect a dispersed crowd. A request to spare neighbouring work can become personal restraint (P03), later explicitly invoked to seek aid (P04). The gameboy speaker names this connection in c0127, rather than leaving the analyst to infer it from adjacency [A01]. Promises follow (P05), with their visible order retained separately [A04]. No quoted promise proves execution or durable protection.

Neighbour relations are not a stage after aggression. Diplomatic assurance and annexation calls overlap; a later offer to move the Czech flag is called greedy. P06 records speakers separating organized partners from rogues, without crediting this distinction with ending attacks. Texas, the T artist and Mindcrack expose different constraints: conditional aid, an immovable crowd and ineffective defense. Those are limits on a simple cooperation story, not exceptions removed from the account.

Two instances show proposals stopping or being withdrawn. The outline speaker names scripted occupation (K02) as the reason it is too late (P09) [A02]. Pat and Mat's speakers discuss changing bots, so automated occupation is not itself a sufficient stopping condition. The Pilsner proposer names a Dutch request (K03) while telling others to abandon the redesign (P09) [A03]. This is accepted local influence; a refused relocation demand elsewhere prevents the stronger claim that any neighbour request commands compliance.

What was left behind also has limits. P07 marks completion and credit, including qualified thanks to difficult neighbours. P08 attempts another canvas after the original clock ends. The later painted flag does not establish transfer of the prior group. The replacement is the odd episode the model must narrate: repeating a mark and arranging shared work again are different achievements. It needs no causal arrow from gratitude to restart. The model relates presences and attributed sequences, not amounts; no coder-stated covariation warrants degree-to-degree arrows.

Dimension projection and mass

The edge inventory, with or without duplicate placements, is identical because there are no extra coder-theme placements. In D01, A02 is internal and A03 enters from D02; no retained edge leaves D01. In D02, A01 and A04 are internal and A03 leaves for D01; no retained edge enters D02. This is the complete unweighted in/out/internal edge mass stated as ID lists, not an empirical prevalence count. P08 remains outside dimensions. The asymmetry describes the selected graph; it is not evidence that the dimensions themselves cause drift, nor that a no-incoming dimension is an exogenous cause.

The mechanisms do not abstract into a single dimension-level dependence. Both an objection and a scripting assessment can constrain a proposal, but their actors and alternatives differ. Therefore D02 to D01 is edge mass only, not a dimension arrow. The arguable recognition member of D02 adds no demonstrated dynamic link.

ungrounded relationships

I expected plan specificity to improve repair, routing to improve coordination, rogue distinctions to preserve pacts, and recognition to encourage continuing contributions. This record supplies plans, channels, distinctions and thanks, but no sufficient linked instance of those effects. These remain undrawn conjectures, not new arrow IDs disguised as evidence. No frequency correlation or base-rate comparison makes them grounded. With these conjectures, a speculative account would connect P02 to P01, P06 to P05 and P07 to P08. Without them, the retained model consists only of A01, A02, A03 and the thin order A04. The claims of successful collective coordination and transfer do not survive removing the conjectures.

Alternatives, counterexamples and coder selection

The strongest rival is accommodation under unequal control. c0223, c0095 and the smaller group's inability to win support it. Yet refusal in c0236, reciprocal commitments in c0129 and participants' proposed bot changes in c0211 prevent reducing all talk to obedience. The model retains both local influence and the inability to compel. A simple harmony model fails on failed aid and hostile overlap; a simple domination model fails to explain refusal and negotiated mutual support.

For A01, coder selection could foreground an explicit reciprocal phrase while omitting unsuccessful appeals. The full gameboy thread verifies that the speaker recalls the dispute; the Texas contrast includes conditional and ineffective aid. That separates an invocation claim from an artifact of coding only successes, without measuring its prevalence.

For A02, the coder may have selected a vivid too-late line and missed continuing outline work. The full outline thread supplies no workaround, weak absence only. Pat and Mat's explicit proposal to change bots is a stronger contrary item than silence; it narrows the mechanism to the outline assessment rather than all scripts.

For A03, a coded withdrawal can overstate a neighbour's authority. The whole Pilsner thread confirms the proposer's directive and records dissent about its meaning. The refused relocation thread shows the same national label does not bind every requester. The difference is a locally accepted objection; stronger consent or command claims remain unsupported.

The contextual coder themes t11 (explaining r/place to people arriving late) and t14 (taunting and insulting across borders) do not enter a higher-tier membership. Their confusion and hostile attribution limit the account, and P06 keeps a related but separately coded attribution practice. No theme is recoded to make it fit. The actual source text remains the test.

Independent disagreement and test reach

The critic's errors corrected sample labels and failure flags, not the quoted grounds or ordering. The external contrasts challenge broader claims about successful repayment, unavoidable scripted defeat and neighbour command. They are not independent falsifications of the narrow reasons retained in A01, A02 and A03. Local falsifiers were sought in the complete source discussions and not observed; that weak absence gives no population assurance. The model explains what speakers give as grounds for these particular acts. Transfer to other episodes remains uncertain.

The rival partition groups access with late explanation, legitimacy with target choice and taunting, aid with shared roads, and endings with replacement attempts; it also separates thanks from documentation. The retained grouping instead distinguishes making work available, choosing its object, negotiating another group's contribution, assessing completed work and trying another undertaking. Late confusion and taunting remain contextual challenges. Recognition and documentation share public assessment rather than a presumed reward effect. D02 remains arguable at that edge; its inclusion supplies no demonstrated causal link. The rival shows that higher grouping is not unique, and the paired reasons in notes-R4.md remain part of this interpretation.
