T4-001: Making a shared object actionable

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Call people to a recognizable flag and specify what or where to contribute.

A contribution needs a referent. Recruitment and specification jointly turn a national presence into something a reader can locate, imagine or add to. The object can be a flag, lettering or another proposed mark, and can remain contested. The grouping does not assume a single inherited goal or completed artifact.

Members: t01, t02.

Question: How is an intended shared object made recognizable enough to invite a contribution?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-002, specify the object or where to find it rather than sustaining the means of execution; against T4-004, do not decide entitlement to others' space.

Variation: Initial calls through concrete letters, coordinates, landmarks, design objections and questions about the next specification.

Not: A claim that all readers accepted one goal, a working tool, an entitlement to territory or an effective leader.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting, calls and specifications never give contributors any common referent and are unrelated slogans or independent private projects.

Dimension: D01.

T4-002: Sustaining distributed execution

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Keep contributions usable through work commitments, tools, correction and current discussion routes.

People support a shared effort by undertaking repeat work and repairing or supplying the means that let dispersed contributions proceed. Human watch, executable scripts, browser corrections and referrals to a current discussion venue are different ways of sustaining execution. A venue referral makes access to discussion available, not its unseen decision or success.

Members: t03, t05, t15, c0107, c0125, c0155, c0177, c0016, c0097, c0098, c0108.

Question: How do dispersed contributions get maintained and made usable without assuming a manager or an effective tool?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-001, sustain execution rather than specify its object; against T4-003, provide working means rather than establish who or what speaks as collective direction.

Variation: Manual care and reciprocal watch through automation, display/link errors, intrusive bots and public referrals to Discord.

Not: Demonstrated deployment of every script, universal compliance, successful off-platform agreement or durable organization.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting the offered tools, repairs and routes bear no relation to making joint contributions possible, and only display technical taste.

Dimension: D01.

T4-003: Giving collective direction a public voice

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Seek, enact, challenge or dissolve a recognizable source of collective directions.

Collective direction becomes publicly addressable through requests for a leader or a current order and through speakers inhabiting or disputing state and military roles. The practical question of whom to ask meets a performed public office, not necessarily an accepted hierarchy. Dissolution withdraws the announced command's standing. Humour and contested authority remain within the variation, not evidence of effective control.

Members: t06, t07, c0205.

Question: How does public discussion make collective direction addressable, and who can claim to speak it?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-002, address the source, standing or currency of direction, not its executable means. Against T4-005, stage or seek direction rather than explain who caused a past conflict.

Variation: Unanswered leadership requests and competing-current-order complaints through mock decrees, ambassadors, parliamentary justification, ceremonial awards and dissolution.

Not: Evidence that a mock president holds enforceable authority, that all office language is serious, or that a dissolution ended every organizing attempt.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting office language neither identifies an addressable collective voice nor invites role-based challenge; it is disconnected costume talk.

Dimension: D01.

T4-004: Regulating territorial ambition

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Propose, bargain over, restrain or relinquish the space a collective effort may take.

Expansion, diplomacy and restraint address a shared problem: how far a flag's intervention may extend in a populated canvas. Taking a neighbour's space, exchanging rebuilding help and releasing an abandoned site are opposed answers to that problem. Their grouping does not turn different speakers into one changing community mind, or claims of permission into proof of settlement.

Members: t04, t09, c0019, c0050, c0039, c0080, c0104, c0105, c0123, c0124, c0130, c0093, c0086, c0113, c0115, c0183, c0186.

Question: What extent of intervention is proposed, negotiated, defended or relinquished?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-001, settle extent in relation to other claims rather than the mark's mere dimensions; against T4-005, address what may be done or exchanged rather than who caused or owes an account of a conflict.

Variation: Unilateral conquest and alternatives through reciprocal assistance, conditional truces, permissions, non-aggression and abandoning a site.

Not: Consensus, a durable treaty, successful rebuilding or a causal progression from aggression to cooperation.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting apparent expansion, restraint and bargains concern unrelated individual marks and do not address competing claims to shared space.

Dimension: D02.

T4-005: Reconstructing agency and responsibility

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Ask or explain who acted, what followed and who owes an answer.

Participants make collective events intelligible by assigning acts and consequences to people or groups, questioning those accounts and proposing accountability. Neutral origin reconstruction and accusations differ in blame, but both make agency legible. Denying that an ally represents a group limits responsibility, while a trial demand extends it into a proposed procedure. The participants' causal explanations remain theirs.

Members: t08, t13, c0219.

Question: Whose actions and commitments can account for the shared outcome, and who can be called to answer?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-004, reconstruct agency or demand an account rather than negotiate territorial terms. Against T4-006, explain or assign the outcome rather than primarily value or reframe experiencing it.

Variation: Neutral origin stories, information questions and explanations through accusations, disavowals, broken-promise disputes and tribunal demands.

Not: An independently established causal history, verified culprit, agreed responsibility or completed tribunal.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting the narratives cannot identify or question any relevant agency or obligation, and accountability proposals do not concern the joint effort.

Dimension: D02.

T4-006: Appraising and reframing joint work

Declaration: coined; source: none. Gloss: Value, lament, preserve or redirect attention from the experience of the effort.

Participants respond to what the joint effort means to them, including pride in working together, gratitude, a lost flag, persistent hurt, parody and diversion. These acts can make the same outcome valuable or painful without agreement between speakers. Appreciation, mourning and amusement are distinct responses to an experienced effort, not phases in an inferred emotional recovery.

Members: t12, c0037, c0038, c0054, c0109, c0141, c0160, c0161, c0178, c0119, c0206, c0207, c0198, c0220, c0131, c0091, c0216, c0217, c0225, c0226.

Question: What value and experience do participants attach to the effort and its ending?

Nearest boundary: Against T4-005, primarily express or reframe experience rather than reconstruct agency or demand an account. Against T4-002, acknowledge a contribution rather than undertake current maintenance.

Variation: Celebrating a completed mark and valuing process through thanks, preserved images, defeat, emptiness, letting go, ironic reward replies, parody and music.

Not: One shared emotion, demonstrated motivation, successful coping, or enduring organizational capacity.

Disconfirmation elsewhere: In another setting the playful and evaluative material is unrelated to participants' experience of the joint effort and only shares adjacency.

No dimension. Its question concerns the experienced value of joint work, not usable shared references or the extent and attribution of collective claims. Do not manufacture a one-theme dimension.
