The puzzle and local explanation

PlaceHearts used a repeatable form that different people could claim, yet its pattern did not identify one body able to direct every placer. Local agreements could organize a named contribution while the field remained contested. The visible record begins with original rows and a known pattern, so it leaves the founding of the collective purpose unseen. The sign proposal gives a purpose for routing: i3q07hRGidzxKrLspP9nQkeV2kI=, c0021, 62puvi, hour 9.9325:

Country and cause requests specify prospective work, P02. Plans and representatives make conversations reachable, P01; published permissions and upkeep duties state what to preserve or change, P03.
