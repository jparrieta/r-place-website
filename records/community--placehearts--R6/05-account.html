Propositions and limits

N01 states the local mechanism of A01: specific preservation terms can turn a neighbour's conditional help into a named contribution by giving that neighbour an arrangement to accept. It rests on words and a dated exchange, not a prevalence tendency; F-001 is its finding. The strongest rival is polite assent unrelated to the terms. 7wNwl5YIuwpJTgnsYrMwlLnjJ7c='s reference to the sectioned area and named background task favours the linked reading. In another setting, an acceptance addressing another object, or rejection despite the alleged agreement, would defeat that reading. The proposition does not promise assent whenever anyone proposes a boundary. It does not cover disputed terms, unwanted collaboration or compliance by people outside the bargain.

N02 states the thin dependency of A02: when participants still seek to continue agreed work but identify failed access as the impediment, they condition resumption on usable access or propose another venue. Its mechanism is the inability to execute the agreed placement on the unreliable connection; F-002 is its finding. Words carry it, with no probability or effect size. The rival is an unrelated reason for stopping; the speaker's explicit connection condition supports the local reading, but an independent eligible negative case is missing. Continued work on the stated failed connection without waiting or changed access, or an explicit different reason for delay, would defeat it. Closure without willingness to continue lies outside this bound.

Both propositions sit at element level inside their respective dimensions. Neither claims every practice under a dimension shares its mechanism. No arrow is theorised or supplied by literature. Their process conditions could recur in voluntary joint work with negotiable objects and contingent access. The record cannot test other settings; imposed command, non-negotiable rights or indifference to continuation may defeat the conditions. Missing chats and one-case coverage limit observation rather than explain behavior. All absence claims remain weak and scoped to the supplied public record.
