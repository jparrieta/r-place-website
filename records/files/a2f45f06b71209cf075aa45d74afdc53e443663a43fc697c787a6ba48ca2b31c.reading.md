## Claim and puzzle

Participants organized through repeated calls, specifications and negotiated commitments, while control over placers and space remained divided. Their work had a recognizable object without a settled specification. The strongest local connections concern speakers' stated reasons for appeals and withdrawal. The public record supports a bounded process account; it does not establish that a unified Czech organization directed every participant or that pacts kept every artwork alive.

The puzzle is how help and joint pictures remain possible while participants cannot bind their own crowd or trust a neighbour's every member. `P01` and `P02` distinguish assembling contributions from settling a design. `K01` preserves the inability to redirect a dispersed crowd. A request to spare neighbouring work can become personal restraint (`P03`), later explicitly invoked to seek aid (`P04`). The gameboy speaker names this connection in `c0127`, rather than leaving the analyst to infer it from adjacency [`A01`]. Promises follow (`P05`), with their visible order retained separately [`A04`]. No quoted promise proves execution or durable protection.

Neighbour relations are not a stage after aggression. Diplomatic assurance and annexation calls overlap; a later offer to move the Czech flag is called greedy. `P06` records speakers separating organized partners from rogues, without crediting this distinction with ending attacks. Texas, the T artist and Mindcrack expose different constraints: conditional aid, an immovable crowd and ineffective defense. Those are limits on a simple cooperation story, not exceptions removed from the account.

Two instances show proposals stopping or being withdrawn. The outline speaker names scripted occupation (`K02`) as the reason it is too late (`P09`) [`A02`]. Pat and Mat's speakers discuss changing bots, so automated occupation is not itself a sufficient stopping condition. The Pilsner proposer names a Dutch request (`K03`) while telling others to abandon the redesign (`P09`) [`A03`]. This is accepted local influence; a refused relocation demand elsewhere prevents the stronger claim that any neighbour request commands compliance.

What was left behind also has limits. `P07` marks completion and credit, including qualified thanks to difficult neighbours. `P08` attempts another canvas after the original clock ends. The later painted flag does not establish transfer of the prior group. The replacement is the odd episode the model must narrate: repeating a mark and arranging shared work again are different achievements. It needs no causal arrow from gratitude to restart. The model relates presences and attributed sequences, not amounts; no coder-stated covariation warrants degree-to-degree arrows.
