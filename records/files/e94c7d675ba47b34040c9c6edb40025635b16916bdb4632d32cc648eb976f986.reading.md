## The claim

Each of the six forms specifies the part of the shared model its name says, and none does the model's work another way. Of the 396 form lines (66 elements and arrows by six forms), 37 specify, 35 restrict, 324 leave the unit untouched, and none replaces it. So the level below specifies the level above without contradicting it. The forms differ in their functioning by which part they change. A changes the terms with neighbours and the plan, C the design, D keeping, help and carrying on, F the spot and the move, B the chat and the scripts, and E the host sub's April Fools play. By the rooms' own word, the forms that changed keeping (D, with C) kept the work alive to the freeze most often. The forms with the chat and the scripts (B), the terms (A) and carrying on (D) coordinated most after it.

**The form I am most sure of: E, The sub's play, run as work.** One element carries it: 56 of its 84 defining rooms add steps to the April Fools frame, against 18.5 per cent of the 558. One branch family explains it: F01's rooms are 54 of the 58 holders, and each subform (F02 to F06) explains a part. The D5 note that F01 and E restate each other is borne out in part (59 of F01's 103 rooms are in E).

**The form I am least sure of: B, Behind the thread: chat, scripts, raids.** Its account called it a grouping, not a form: its three parts are held by different rooms at about chance. It changes 7 units, the fewest, and its distinctness lies mostly in how often it holds the elements of identity and contention, which the rule does not test.

**The form that best supports the claim: D, Keeping, with help or carrying on.** It changes 17 units, the most. Every change is added detail or a condition, and ten branches have lines in it (F04, F11 to F14, F18, F22 to F24, F27).

**The form that most threatens it: E.** Ten of its 13 changes are restrictions, eight of them on arrows, and many say the record cannot show the order. Its records hold the play and the reports of the canvas work, and less of the work between them. If a form's restrictions track how thin its record of the canvas work is, the forms partly sort records, not organizing.

**The condition between them** is whether a form's record holds the canvas work between the call and the report. Where it does, the form specifies the model where it works (A, C, D, F). Where it does not, the form restricts the model's arrows (E).
