## Fit between claim and validation

The propositions are words claims resting on source statements, not quantities about commonness. Their gates are the stated local purpose, policy and motive conditions, not prevalence classifiers. The empirical arrows remain codes-read, not reading-alone, and no mechanism came from a literature replacement. `N03` remains thin because the evidence cannot discriminate reported motive from retrospective justification.

All cross-episode reviews remain in-sample. No independent case-return verdict or population comparison has occurred. Procedural file checks can resolve IDs, quotes, inventory and scope declarations; they cannot establish construct validity, comprehension or causal truth.

## What would change this account

A source showing the example addressed another object, the removal gave a contradictory reason, or the helper retracted the restoration account would require revising its proposition and held finding. Evidence tying particular bot reports to the same implementation would resolve an explicitly unknown relation. Destination records linking contributors, tasks, information and inducements could support a more complete undertaking, but they are not present in this archive. No claim depends on their absence being proof of nonexistence.
