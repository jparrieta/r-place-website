## T4-001: Defining a task worth doing

Coined. Gloss: Which object, extent or priority should guide the next work?

Participants make the object of work disputable. A proposed drawing, a limit on expansion and a cancellation each ask others to orient to a specified undertaking; shared national imagery does not settle which version deserves their effort.

Gate: A turn proposes, contests, chooses, changes or cancels the shared undertaking, its desired end-state, extent or relative priority.

Variation: Images, invasions, restraint and room decoration can all be proposed; choices range from new projects to narrowing or cancelling one.

Disconfirm elsewhere: The purported choices would be only relays of an uncontested task, with no proposals, competing end-states or cancellation.

## T4-002: Making room conditional

Coined. Gloss: On what terms may another party remain, expand, or receive protection?

Participants make a claim to space answerable to other people. Pleas for survival, unequal threats and reciprocal repairs all address what one party owes or permits another. The conditions differ and can conflict; the theme does not imply fair bargaining or consent.

Gate: A turn requests, grants, refuses or contests permission, restraint, peace, protection or reciprocal obligation between parties around occupied canvas space.

Variation: Grounds include kindness, prior presence, kinship, force and reciprocal pixels; permission can be offered, disputed or refused.

Disconfirm elsewhere: Protection and coexistence would occur without any request, entitlement claim, refusal or obligation; references to friendship would only decorate unrelated acts.

## T4-003: Making contributions fit

Coined. Gloss: How can separate contributions follow, sustain or correct an undertaking?

Separate pixels can undo one another. Instructions, warnings and admissions address whether contributions fit the current task and other contributions. The same problem appears in rehearsed attacks, routine upkeep and painters mistaking a new image for damage. The theme preserves failed attempts and uncertain uptake.

Gate: A turn recruits, alerts, schedules, commits, hands over, reports ongoing work, supplies a tool or template, or repairs understanding for contributions to an identifiable undertaking.

Variation: Timed attacks, upkeep across sleep, scripts, resources, recognition advice and admitted mistakes differ in detail; an attempt can have no observed uptake.

Disconfirm elsewhere: The alleged alignment turns would neither solicit, specify, report nor correct contributions, or all work would be independently complete with no shared undertaking.

## T4-004: Claiming where discussion belongs

Coined. Gloss: Where can this work be discussed, and who can direct or access that discussion?

Participants decide who should meet where while moderators also regulate the main room. A venue can support planning, exclude a speaker or keep event traffic elsewhere. The record separates an instruction to move from knowing the destination and from actually moving; it does not give moderators command of every pixel.

Gate: A turn creates, solicits access to, routes people toward, enforces or contests an event-discussion venue or its authority and visibility.

Variation: Participant-created channels and moderator referrals differ in authority; replies range from acceptance to ignorance of a venue, challenge or reported exclusion.

Disconfirm elsewhere: All venue mentions would be incidental URLs, with no request for access, creation, routing, exclusion or dispute over where discussion belongs.

## T4-005: Accounting for a shared event

Coined. Gloss: What do participants say happened, who deserves credit or blame, and what is worth preserving?

Participants turn distributed activity into accounts of what their collective did. Credit, accusations and preserved records make its meaning contestable even after placement ends. This explains why a report of success and a harmed neighbour's account can coexist; it does not reconcile them or show a collective change of mind.

Gate: A turn recounts, evaluates, celebrates, accuses, thanks, commemorates or preserves the event and its resulting relationships or collective work.

Variation: Live victory claims and later memories coexist; allies praise protection while others accuse Sweden of destruction. Humour can sustain affiliation or ridicule a claim.

Disconfirm elsewhere: The alleged accounts would contain no evaluation, recollection, credit, blame or preservation of a shared event, only unrelated entertainment or current task instructions.
