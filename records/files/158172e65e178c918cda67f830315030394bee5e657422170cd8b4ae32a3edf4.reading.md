## Propositions and limits

`N01` states the local mechanism of `A01`: specific preservation terms can turn a neighbour's conditional help into a named contribution by giving that neighbour an arrangement to accept. It rests on words and a dated exchange, not a prevalence tendency; `F-001` is its finding. Its mechanism is the inability to execute the agreed placement on the unreliable connection; `F-002` is its finding. Words carry it, with no probability or effect size. Continued work on the stated failed connection without waiting or changed access, or an explicit different reason for delay, would defeat it. Closure without willingness to continue lies outside this bound.

Both propositions sit at element level inside their respective dimensions. Neither claims every practice under a dimension shares its mechanism. No arrow is theorised or supplied by literature. Their process conditions could recur in voluntary joint work with negotiable objects and contingent access. The record cannot test other settings; imposed command, non-negotiable rights or indifference to continuation may defeat the conditions. Missing chats and one-case coverage limit observation rather than explain behavior. All absence claims remain weak and scoped to the supplied public record.
