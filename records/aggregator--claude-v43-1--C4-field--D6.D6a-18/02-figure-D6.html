The figure: the model with its forms inside it

Drawn at D6b from fractal-forms-D6.jsonl by _analysis/d6b_figure.py. It is the stage C5 figure (figure-C5.md, drawing 1: 21 boxes, 20 arrows, each arrow with the proposition that carries it) with the six room forms drawn into its boxes and arrows, at the level where they differ: the element or arrow. Each box and arrow names the forms that specify it (add detail that holds in their rooms) or restrict it (the form's restrict lines lean: the unit holds there only under a condition, or, for many arrows, the rooms' lines say it fails there or that the record cannot show it). A box with no form named is held by every form as the model states it. Four elements outside the C5 drawing are added because a form changes them (P19, P23, P27, P32); the claim C01 stands beside the figure, as at C5. No form replaces any element, but the coding vocabulary made replacing nearly unreachable (retelling-D6.md, the frame). Most of the changes drawn lean only at the rule's own thresholds: 20 of the 72 survive ratio 1.5 and floor 15 per cent (form-cells-sensitivity-D6.tsv). The unvalidated proposals (proposals-D6.jsonl) are not drawn. Redrawn at D6a-09 with this paragraph and the legend's line on B corrected; the drawing and the tables are unchanged.

Legend

  • Boxes. Left, the conditions; middle, what the room and its members do; right, what follows the work. The bar at hour 73 marks the close. Shaded boxes are changed by at least one form; grey boxes by none.
  • specified by X: in form X, add-step, add-condition or link lines on the unit lean (held by at least the larger of 5 and a tenth of X's defining rooms, at 1.2 times the 558 defining rooms' share or more). restricted by X: restrict lines lean and outnumber the other leaning ops. The rule was logged at D6a-01 before it ran (_analysis/d6_fractal.py).
  • Arrows. Each keeps its C5 label: its id and the proposition (N01 to N09) that carries it, then the forms that specify or restrict it. Of the 20 arrows, one is specified by a form (A05, by B and D), and 16 are restricted by one or more. A restriction says the order holds in that form only under a condition, or that the record there cannot show it.
  • Forms. A to F are the six room forms of D4a-08c, by their final names (room-forms-names.md). B is a residual grouping, not a form: three facets (the chat, the scripts and the raids) held by different rooms at about chance overlap. The 35 branches are the level below and are not drawn; the table names the branches that explain each form's changes (fractal-D6.jsonl).

What the figure leaves out

It leaves out what the C5 figure left out (its last section), the eleven unvalidated proposals, the two held proposals (QD4-001; stated in full in retelling-D6.md), and the rates at which the forms hold an element without changing it, which the rule does not test (told in the retelling under D05 and D08). It does not draw the branches, the splits of form D, or the survival reading by form, which the retelling gives in words.
