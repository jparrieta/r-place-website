This is not a claim that recruitment caused a particular redesign. What varies is whether contributors lower immediate demands, imagine later growth, request help, commit or celebrate progress; doubts and unmet requests remain part of this conditional ambition.

Question: How does a recognizable ambition become work that available contributors can join and maintain?
