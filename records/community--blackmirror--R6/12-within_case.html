Stopped early: E-06, proposing a Tesla-A change

The entire supplied thread 631ycf contains one item, posted by B9HkOZOsK7Fii25teAo4hVpZnkc= at H52.25833333333333: “Let's white bearify the A in the tesla logo at 902,204” (c0055, cite2964). The operation, target and coordinates are concrete. “Let's” invites joint action rather than simply reporting an already completed change. The White Bear reference links the proposal culturally to the room's other pixel work, but that vocabulary does not establish operational continuity.

This is a distinct proposed target, not a recorded continuation of the array-defense exchange. Nothing in the item says who will join, supplies a pattern, reports a first placement, negotiates with Tesla placers or answers a request for clarification. Those omissions describe the supplied item and the limits of this thread. They cannot establish that nobody acted elsewhere, that the target was hostile, that there was insufficient time or that a design failed. A precise location alone is not an observed group undertaking.

The first and last observed incident therefore have the same instrument hour. That equality is an observation bracket, not the duration of attempted work. The supplied record ends at the proposal. Calling this an abandoned project would add an event. Calling it successful extension of the array would add a connection and outcome. The stopped-early kind is available here only as a course that stops in the record, and that qualification must follow it into the model.

Compared with E-02, this episode lacks the explicit “Good idea” adoption and the later request/answer pair. Compared with E-03, it lacks recorded endorsement of a modified approach. Those descriptive contrasts limit claims that specifying a recognizable object automatically mobilizes helpers. They do not identify why reception is unobserved. The episode remains meaningful as an actionable proposal the explanation must leave outside demonstrated uptake, rather than erase, assimilate to collective work or turn into a failure.
