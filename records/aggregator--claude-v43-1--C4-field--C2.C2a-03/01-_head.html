The method question, in one sentence

Can independently reconstructed case histories be composed into a shared dynamic account that supports explanations at successive levels of specificity, from population-wide patterns, through forms and subforms, to particular organizational histories, while keeping access to the evidence from which those explanations were developed?

The theory question is unchanged: when strangers organize on a clock they did not set, with no way to compel anyone and no goal given to them, what forms does organizing take, how do they differ in their functioning, and which of them keep the work alive?

The current account, and the contrary rooms

Pass 3's model keeps pass 2's spine, branches and close, and adds the host's line. A member starts a mark, calls gather hands, the hands build, and keeping the mark becomes the work. The calls fill the host sub, and the sub draws a line around them: one thread, removals, a canvas sub. Keeping the mark brings the room up against neighbours: it talks with them, a room short of hands turns to other rooms for help, and rooms under a common threat join coalitions. A call can also turn hands against a rival's work; most such calls are answered inside the room. A room that judges it cannot hold its spot moves, and keeps calling. The close runs in order: celebrated, recorded, carried on. Some rooms have no readers before the canvas: side subs a readership opens, and bodies the canvas gathers. They run the same steps from their founding.

The room that best supports the account is stevenuniverse, drawn by seed at pass 3 and told whole in the story's terms, from its first call to its record, with borders, truces, refused attacks and the host's megathread between. The room that most threatens it is still greenlattice: the canvas gathered it as a colour faction, it ruled on other rooms' applications to build, and its members split over whether the lattice was art to keep pure or a home for guests. The condition between them is whether the room's own membership decides whom it fights and whom it admits. A readership that brings a work to the canvas is told. A body whose faith, history or ideology picks its rival and rules on its guests is not yet told.
