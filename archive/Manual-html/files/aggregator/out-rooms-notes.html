out/rooms/ (room notes)

One note per room: the analyst's prior, dated.

What it holds

A1 fixes the form, under 350 words: a header (# r/<sub>, read date, coded share, off-platform pointers), then doing, week, named, goal, unsettled, coder unsure (what the coder said she could not place, with thread ids), carrying (three concept ids), not this, ended, struck by (the one free line, never cited), and made, written last from the canvas. A later stage appends one dated later: line. STANDING §2 also places D2's model-D.md, verdict-D and placements in rooms/<sub>/.

Who writes it, and when

The analyst at A1, one note per room of the analysed set, in the order of out/order-A1.txt (mem.py next names the next room), written "before you open the next room" and logged. The harness refuses a note whose room's concepts were not read (mem.py codes --sub), and checks the note's form the moment it is written.

Why it exists

STANDING §1: "Your room notes are your prior"; they are "queried (mem.py note <sub>), never read whole as a list", "never a source for a quote", and each later stage says what it found that they did not predict. A1: "a room you read and did not write about is a room you have not read."

Where

out/rooms/.

Checked against: stages/STAGE-A1-reading-room-notes.md, STANDING-INSTRUCTIONS.md §1 and §2, tools/mem.py.
