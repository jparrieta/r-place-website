The verbs and options added or changed in the v38 tools pass.

Who writes it, and when

Written with the tools pass; shipped in the analyst's tools/.

Why it exists

Its heading says it is "for merging into STANDING §1, HOW-TO-RUN.md and the stage files". STANDING §1 carries the list, and says where it and the older list differ, it wins.
