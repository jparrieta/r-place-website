B1: Coding: third-order scheme

Model: gpt-6-astra. Effort: a: high, b: high, c: xhigh, d: high, e: xhigh.

Use one leg at a time in a study. Each leg continues the previous native checkpoint. Reader jobs and separate studies can run in parallel. Closure automatically validates, archives and dispatches. Repeat counts and pilot rounds below are starting examples.
