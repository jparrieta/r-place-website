The changelog (CHANGELOG-v32.md)

What moved between versions of the coder instrument, and why.

What it holds

v31 to v32 (2026-09-09): emit.thread refuses a read out_of_scope disposition of a thread with ten or more in-window items unless excluded_quote is what cite returned; C5 counts in-window items from coder_corpus.jsonl. v30 to v31 (2026-09-08): close_run.py --preflight, C11 (a coded thread keeps a live code), C12 (branch-B notes repeat the gate), and a guard on branch-B raw reads before step 4. Earlier history: v29's case lead and two branches, the forest, the facts files, the audit, and the runs that tested them.

Who writes it, and when

The instrument's author, at each version change. Dates are given per section.

Why it exists

The instrument README says: "CHANGELOG-v32.md says what moved and when". The v32 section gives the reason: an audit "found a systematic C5 failure in completed v30 rooms". The rationale beside the instrument, rationale/V32-C5-PREVENTION-RATIONALE.md, gives the same reason and adds that the method, evidence standard and outputs did not change.
