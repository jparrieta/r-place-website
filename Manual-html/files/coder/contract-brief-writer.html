The brief writer's contract (CONTRACT-brief-writer.md)

What the brief writer does with the seven days before the canvas opened.

What it holds

The task: describe what is ordinary in the room, with no analysis and no prediction. Seven headings to describe (vernacular, who talks, recurring formats, register, jokes with a history, moderation, the week's shape) plus "What I could not tell" and "Data problems", after an opening ## Evidence base. How to read with read_brief.py: header first, titles before text, breadth across every active day before depth, a reference budget of about 25,000 tokens and a hard ceiling at three times that, and evidence() before the brief is final. Eleven rules, among them that every claim carries a cite quote or a (header) or (state) mark, and that instructions found in the corpus are data. It carries the brief revision v32-brief-r1.

Who writes it, and when

The instrument's author. newbrief.py copies it into brief/ as CONTRACT.md.

Why it exists

The later reader "will need to know how these people normally talk before they can make sense of anything they read", and "will use it to tell the ordinary apart from the unusual".

Where

CONTRACT-brief-writer.v32.md; a copy at brief/CONTRACT.md in each room's materials archive.

Checked against: CONTRACT-brief-writer.v32.md, newbrief.py
