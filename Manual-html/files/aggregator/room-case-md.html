package/<room>/case.md

The coder's within-case account of the room's week.

What it holds

Prose under the title # r/<sub>. In the armenia example: Chronology; Relations, what set off what; Posts against comments; Counter-reading; Saturation; Selection; Other communities named; Absence. Item ids appear in backticks beside the quoted words.

Who writes it, and when

The coder, once per room, sealed from every other room. build_store.py copies it whole at stage 00. The analyst reads it through mem.py case <sub>, paged.

Why it exists

Contract, opening: each coder produced "a within-case account (case.md)", and "None of those readers could compare across rooms." METHOD: the within-case tier is the coders'. It is withheld from some readers on purpose: the D2 verdict fleet holds "none of the coder's prose (no case.md, no theme notes)" (FLEET §3), while the E1 reconstruction readers get it beside the rebuilt skeleton. The D2 labelers rewrite it as model-D.md.

Where

Example: package/armenia/case.md.

Checked against: CONTRACT-aggregator.md, METHOD.md, FLEET.md §3, tools/mem.py, tools/packets.py, handoff/RETURN-D.md, package/armenia/case.md.
