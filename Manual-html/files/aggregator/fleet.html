FLEET.md

The fan-out to readers, the figures, and the return.

What it holds

§1 The fan-out: fleets at B2 to C1 as hands, at R = 2 readers per item; the pilot, fleet, adjudication and revision steps and the files each writes; the three legs of every fleet stage, with the operator's fleet.py as the dispatch; the 50 KB packet ceiling. §2 The figures: one definition each for fidelity, agreement, gold fidelity, transmission after revision, stability, the position curve and transmission, and "Fidelity is primary and agreement secondary." §3 The return, D1 to D3: the handoff folder, the labelers in Codex, ingest.py, and the verdict fleet with its criterion (composes, composes-with-residue, does-not-compose, cannot-tell).

Who writes it, and when

Written with the instrument. build.sh copies it to the root at build; AGENT.md says it "comes with the fleet stages".

Why it exists

Contract §4: seven stages send work to readers who are not the analyst, and no reader runs on the analyst's model or in its session. FLEET says what the figures license: they bound "whether the written instrument decides, never whether it is right."

Where

FLEET.md; source aggregator/instrument/FLEET.md.

Checked against: FLEET.md, CONTRACT-aggregator.md §4, AGENT.md, harness/build.sh.
