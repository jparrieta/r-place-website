# Where the member lists are

The study's two lists, made 27 August 2026, on its Drive (`venue/` in the Layer A data folder). Download them into this folder (the `confirm=t` part skips Google's large-file page):

    curl -L -o _pre_progress.jsonl "https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=1LoKgxHHoyEnVcLAovkgap5xesZzB5cIn&export=download&confirm=t"
    curl -L -o _win_progress.jsonl "https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=1uk_RQluR77iYF2hhnF47GCkNr04IJ9d2&export=download&confirm=t"

11,915,307 and 36,401,733 bytes; 763 lines each. The pre-window list holds Reddit usernames; treat it as the data (the Manual's terms of use).
