# Interview or continue research?

## Ask about a saved state

Use [checkpoint interviews](../interviews.html). A verified bundle combines the saved conversation with its historical files. The interview runs separately; the research stage and the original record stay as they were.

The catalog separates **Read the record**, **Download and interview** and **Interview online**, and marks any bundle or hosted route that is unavailable. A transcript shows what was said; a complete restore needs the verified bundle.

[Interview guide](interviews.html) · [Full restore and service instructions](../runtime.md)

## Continue or branch the analysis

This is a task for the authors or a handler. Use the original handler and its recorded checkpoint, platform and run identity. The handler checks the restoration and the stage boundary before any research turn begins.

Follow the [leg controller](../sources/legs.html) and [handler protocol](../sources/handler.html) for Claude, or the [Codex runtime guide](../sources/codex.html) for Codex. Read the relevant [full stage card](aggregator.html#stage-map) before continuing.

Launching, resuming or branching research happens outside this website.
