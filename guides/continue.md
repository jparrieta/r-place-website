# Interview or continue research?

## Ask about a saved state

Use [checkpoint interviews](../interviews.html). A verified bundle combines the saved conversation with its historical files. The interview runs separately; it does not advance a research stage or change the original record.

The catalogue distinguishes **Read the record**, **Download and interview**, and **Interview online**. An unavailable bundle or hosted route is labelled. A transcript alone does not prove a complete restore exists.

[Interview guide](interviews.html) · [Full restore and service instructions](../runtime.md)

## Continue or branch the analysis

This is an operator task. Use the original handler and its recorded checkpoint, platform and run identity. The handler checks the restoration and the stage boundary before any research turn begins.

Follow the [leg controller](../sources/legs.html) and [handler protocol](../sources/handler.html) for Claude, or the [Codex runtime guide](../sources/codex.html) for Codex. Read the relevant [full stage card](aggregator.html#stage-map) before continuing.

The website does not launch, resume or branch live research.
