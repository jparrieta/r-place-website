# Model organisms across fields

Why keep returning to one fly, one game or one set of images? A common system gives a new study something to build on and something to answer to. Researchers can ask what an account adds, corrects or explains that earlier accounts could not.

The fruit fly is a biological model organism. Chess is a model environment for psychology. ImageNet2012 is a benchmark for computer vision. They illustrate different ways to accumulate knowledge around a common system. We propose r/place as a model organism for organization science.

## Four pillars, four settings

These pillars are our way of comparing the cases, informed by [Ankeny and Leonelli](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.shpsa.2010.11.039). They are not a checklist those authors applied to all four. On a small screen, scroll the table sideways to compare fields.

| Pillar | Fruit fly: biology | ImageNet2012: computer vision | Chess: cognitive psychology | r/place: organization science |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A common system to study** | Standardized fly strains that laboratories can obtain, breed and examine. | A defined image collection, 1,000 categories and common training, validation and test sets. | A game with explicit rules, recorded positions and measurable differences in player skill. | A shared canvas and placement rules, preserved through pixel changes and participants' written exchanges across subreddits. |
| **Connected phenomena** | Genetics, development, physiology and behaviour can be investigated in the same organism. | Classification and localization examine related aspects of object recognition, within specified tasks. | Perception, memory, learning and search contribute to explaining expertise in the same task environment. | Coordination, maintenance, conflict and alliances can be studied within the same event. Subreddit boundaries need not define every organization. |
| **Shared means of investigation** | Stock centres distribute strains; databases such as FlyBase connect findings and research resources. | Images, annotations, development kits and evaluation rules support comparisons between methods. | Published position tasks, game records and computational models allow earlier findings to be revisited. | Collection scripts, coding contracts, analysis stages and checkpoints expose how an account was produced. The current application selects 705 communities. |
| **Knowledge to build on** | Findings about the fly contribute to connected biological explanations and comparisons with other organisms. | New methods face common tests. Better benchmark performance is evidence about those tasks. | New theories must address established findings about skill, perception and memory, with their domain limits. | New accounts can extend or correct the baseline. Claims beyond r/place and comparisons with later editions remain possibilities to investigate. |

## What the comparison shows

The forms of accumulation differ. ImageNet makes performance comparable through agreed labels and scores. Chess and the fruit fly support explanations of connected processes. Our proposal for r/place is to build and revise explanations against a common record, while keeping the instrument open to change.

The record does not prescribe the unit of analysis. One study can examine 705 curated communities; another can trace alliances or newly formed coordination groups. Both must show how their claims relate to the evidence and to earlier accounts. Preserving more records enlarges what can be investigated, but does not itself produce a better explanation.
