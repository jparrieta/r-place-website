# What this publication contains

Updated 29 October 2026. This page describes the recorded run and keeps its checks apart from the work proposed next.

The study's frame contains 705 communities. Community coding produces codes, second-order themes and case accounts. The aggregator compares this material, builds an account of organizing and returns the model to the community coders. The census decisions are recorded with the data, and downloading the data does not repeat them.

The run is finished. Its final publication, `f27566528a51`, records all 18 stages closed, ending at E2b on 28 October 2026. Earlier checkpoints keep their own source commits and saved-state dates. A finished run shows that every stage closed; whether its explanation is correct is the question the checks below address.

## How the model is checked

An account can read well and still misread the record. The paper groups its checks at three levels.

### Coding: does the account fit its evidence?

Scripts check cited quotations against source items. A resolved quotation shows that the text exists; whether the reading of it is sound is a separate question. The paper also reports paired coding of 300 communities with two models. This site does not yet present a scored comparison of those codings, and the count is no estimate of reliability.

### Composition: do claims hold across cases?

Human audits can test selected inferences across cases. The run also kept critical readings, returned its model to the community coders, and checked how well local accounts could be reconstructed. [r/PlaceNL's return](../results/placenl.html#repair-and-change) shows a revision and its limits; [E1](../stages/E1.html) explains the reconstruction check.

Two further population models are planned for comparison: induction supported by an algorithm from term frequencies, with no coding agent, and a separate aggregator that works from tables across cases. Both are future work.

### Source: what does another view reveal?

The central r/place subreddit falls outside the frame of 705 communities. At [C4](../records/aggregator--claude-v43-1--C4-field--C4.C4a.html), the Field agent's interim reading of it prompted tentative additions and qualifications to the community-based model. Finishing that analysis and comparing it with the population model remain future work.
