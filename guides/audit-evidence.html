Audit the evidence

Use five questions from TRACE to inspect this study. This page maps the evidence to those questions; it is neither a TRACE certification nor a new protocol. Records show what was kept, and whether an explanation is sound is a separate judgment.

The example is one C2 model-building step in the indexed publication, commit c070d990be882. These records come from the parent run, before the C4 Field branch, and cover that step only. The full instructions and source hashes can be read on this site; instructions say what should happen, and the records show what did.
