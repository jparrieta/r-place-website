# Terms used on this site

The words this site uses, as the paper and the instrument's contract define them. The agents' own files sometimes use older words; where they differ, the older word is given.

## The study

**Community.** One subreddit studied as a case: its members, the artworks it built and what its members wrote during the event and the day after. The agents' files call a community a *room*.

**Dataset.** The public record of the event: pixel changes, posts and comments. It is frozen.

**Instrument.** The harness in which agents read the dataset, with its contracts, stages and checks. It can change; later runs are judged by what they add.

**Run.** One use of the instrument from stage A1 to E2. This site shows the run `claude-v43-1`, often written v43.

## The agents

**Community coder.** The agent that reads one community's full record and codes its organizing. It writes the first two layers of coding and the community's case account. For a large community, **fleet coders** read batches of threads for it.

**Aggregator.** The agent that composes the coded communities into the population model. It builds the last three layers of coding, the model and the organizational forms.

**Field agent.** A separate agent that reads the central r/place forum. At stage C4 it supplied an interim view; its own analysis is not finished.

**Handler.** Opens and closes the stages in order and checks what they deliver. It does not interpret the data.

## Layers of coding

The *ladder* is the five layers from quote to aggregate dimension. Construction runs upward.

| Layer | What it is | Written by |
|---|---|---|
| T1 first-order concepts | members' words, coded, with the quote | community coder |
| T2 second-order themes | one community's concepts consolidated within the case | community coder |
| T3 third-order themes | kinds of second-order theme across communities, in the material's words | aggregator |
| T4 fourth-order themes | named groups of third-order themes; constructs | aggregator |
| Aggregate dimensions, D01 to D08 | what the fourth-order themes are about; constructs | aggregator |

## Results

**Population model.** The aggregator's qualitative model of the 705 curated communities: its parts, arrows and telling.

**Organizational forms.** The six groups A to F that the aggregator cut from the communities' changes to the model and their codes.

**Return.** The model goes back to every community coder, who answers with its community's record (stage D2); the revised model goes back again (D3).

## Running the instrument

**Stage.** One step of the run, A1 to E2, with its own card and outputs. A stage is one or more legs.

**Leg.** One launch of an agent within a stage.

**Handback.** The file in which a stage records what it needs, what is wrong with the instrument and its close.

**Checkpoint.** A leg closed at a named point, saved and resumable.

**Checkpoint interview.** A conversation with a saved agent restored at a checkpoint. It answers from the state it held then.
