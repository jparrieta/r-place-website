The example is one C2 model-building step in the indexed publication, commit `c070d990be882`. These records come from the parent run, before the C4 Field branch, and cover that step only. The [full instructions](../sources/contract.html) and [source hashes](../sources/manifest.json) can be read on this site; instructions say what should happen, and the records show what did.

The [evidence register](../evidence.json) lists exact file versions, hashes, checks and limits. It records this inspection; acceptance is decided elsewhere.
