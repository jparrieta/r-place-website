# From evidence to explanation

The instrument moves between local accounts and a model that must explain their differences. Its contracts govern the work; these pages explain the route.

[Agents and their roles](agent-roles.html) explains who prepares the material, reads it, builds the model and checks the work.

## First, understand each community

Community coders code participants' words, with fleet coders in large communities. Each community coder brings together the codes, themes and a case account. Exact quotations and reading records let a reviewer check the evidence behind an account.

[How community coding works](communities.html) · [Full community coder contract](../sources/coder-contract.html)

## Then compare, build and challenge

A1 reads the cases before a common scheme is named. B1 to B4 build, pilot, apply and adjudicate that scheme. Independent readings make disagreements visible, and adjudication stays an interpretive task.

C1 to C5 build the fourth-order themes, the dynamic model and its account. The model is challenged, compared with the literature, and set against the Field account when one is supplied.

The Field agent reads the central r/place subreddit separately. C4 compares the account it supplies with the community-based model and records its scope and limits. In v43, the Field agent supplied an interim reading and comments before its own analysis was finished, and a comparison with its completed account is still pending. If no account is supplied, C4 records its absence. This site has no launch guide for the Field agent yet.

D1 to D5 return the model to communities, assess corrections and build the taxonomy. D6 retells the whole model with the organizational forms inside it; new ideas stay unvalidated proposals. A local difference can stand in one community's account, and the global model need not explain every case the same way.

E1 audits the work. E2 reviews the handling and proposes the next version of the instrument. Checks show what was read and written; the explanation rests on evidence and argument.

## Review an inference

Follow a claim to its account, codes and source material. Compare the aggregator's record with the alternatives and limits it states. A checkpoint interview can probe that interpretation; its answers are new, given from the saved state.
