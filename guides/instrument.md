# From evidence to explanation

The instrument moves between local accounts and a model that must explain their differences. Its contracts govern the work; these pages explain the route.

[Agents and their roles](agent-roles.html) explains who prepares the material, reads it, builds the model and checks the work.

## First, understand each community

Community readers code participants’ words. A case lead consolidates codes, themes and a case account. Exact quotations and reading records let a reviewer check the evidence behind an account.

[How community coding works](communities.html) · [Full coder contract](../sources/coder-contract.html)

## Then compare, build and challenge

**A1 reads the cases before a common scheme is named. B1 to B4 build, pilot, apply and adjudicate that scheme.** Independent readings make disagreements visible; adjudication remains an interpretive task.

**C1 to C5 develop higher-order themes, the dynamic model and its account.** The model is challenged, compared with literature, and examined against the Field account when supplied.

The **Field analysis** studies the central r/place forum separately. C4 compares the account supplied with the community model, recording its scope and limits. In v43, the Field agent supplied an interim reading and comments; its full model was not finished. A comparison with that completed model remains pending. If no account is supplied, C4 records its absence. This site does not yet provide a standalone Field launch guide.

**D1 to D5 return the model to communities, assess corrections and develop the taxonomy. D6 retells the whole model with the organizational forms inside it; new insights remain unvalidated proposals.** A difference can matter within one community without requiring the global model to explain every case identically.

**E1 and E2 audit the work and examine feedback.** File checks support the inquiry; evidence and argument carry the explanation.

## Review an inference

Follow a claim to its account, codes and source material. Compare the analyst’s record with its stated alternatives and limits. A checkpoint interview can probe that interpretation, but its answers are new responses from the saved state.
