# How the aggregator works

The aggregator compares the coded communities and builds the population model under researcher supervision. It reads the community coders' sealed work, never the source record. The model then goes back to every community coder, who answers from its community's record; these returns are not interviews with participants. Finally, the aggregator cuts organizational forms from the changes communities need.

The aggregator owns the interpretation. Checks establish what was read, written and preserved; they do not establish that an explanation is true.
