Dates

Counts

Short version Layers of coding

In detail Elsewhere on the site

Each of the 705 communities was read by its own community coder, who saw no other community. Their codes and themes are what the aggregator reads.

Layers of coding

Layers of coding. The community coders write T1 first-order concepts, in members’ words, and T2 second-order themes within their community. The aggregator builds T3 third-order themes across communities, T4 fourth-order themes and the aggregate dimensions D01 to D08. The agents’ own text calls a community a room. All terms
