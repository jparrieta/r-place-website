Output

Counts
16,559,897 pixel changes by 1,166,924 accounts; 7.5 million posts and comments in 2,796 subreddits; 1,473 artworks; 705 communities with 2,130,302 posts and comments

Short version Who counts as a community

In detail Elsewhere on the site

Of the 2,796 subreddits the atlas credits with an artwork, 705 stayed: their members wrote about the event while the canvas was open.

Who counts as a community

A credited subreddit stays if its members wrote about the event while the canvas was open. Each judgement is recorded with its evidence and can be reversed. Seventy-eight of the 705, like r/PlaceNL, have no record before the event.
