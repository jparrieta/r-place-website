One community: PlaceNL

Follow one community from its public record and coding to its local model, its contribution to the population model, and its place among the final organizational forms.

What r/PlaceNL was

r/PlaceNL did not exist before the canvas opened. Its record starts at H27:40 with a post calling itself “the official coordination for placing pixels in Place”, and runs to the day after the freeze. In that time it published hourly plans, sent its planners to Discord, ran maintenance scripts, made pacts and broke them, handed orange ground back to communities it had displaced, and fought with itself over a Yeti. It wrote in Dutch and English. Its neighbours in the Dutch area were r/thenetherlands, the standing national sub, and r/cirkeltrek, its circlejerk.
