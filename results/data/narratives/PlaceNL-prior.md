## 1. What I expect this community to have done

I expect to find organizing already underway when the substantive public record begins. An official plan and directions to Discord will show an effort to make scattered placers act on shared targets. I do not expect this record to show how the group first formed or chose its earliest purpose. A subreddit, a Dutch identity and a claimed orange area do not by themselves establish one organization or one agreed goal.

My main expectation is that participants made collective action possible through plans, live communication, scripts and negotiated agreements, while their ability to direct everyone remained limited. A published plan could guide maintenance and restraint without governing every person placing Dutch pixels. I expect public statements distinguishing organized participants from independent placers. The uncertainty is how much the linked exchanges actually show action following directions, rather than organizers describing their own reach.

I expect the information problem to involve the age and location of instructions. Retained coding suggests that live work moved into Discord while Reddit carried copies, questions and corrections. I expect participants to explain conflicts by stale plans or missed updates. That explanation alone will not establish that moving venues caused a particular mistake. The missing Discord record limits what I can say about decisions made there.

Scripts should complicate any simple contrast between coordination and disorder. I expect evidence of successful repair and maintenance, alongside objections that scripts resisted new designs or carried out disputed removal. Access to the person able to revise a script may matter. I expect the same technical means to help preserve an agreed target and obstruct a proposed change. This requires linked evidence about the target and permissions; it cannot follow from the presence of the word script.

I expect relations with neighbors to include restraint, bargains, offers to rebuild and conditional use of claimed territory. Conquest and accommodation may occur together. A proposal to lend space while retaining a territorial claim differs from giving it away. Promises and accepted offers may establish commitments even when the record cannot establish their completion.

The Yeti dispute is likely to challenge an account of a unified group protecting its work against outsiders. My retained reading places conflicting Dutch positions inside the dispute. I expect arguments about preservation and removal, attempts to supply or restore a design, and uncertainty about who caused the reported result. Disagreement among speakers will not establish that the community changed its mind.

I expect placement closure to interrupt unfinished work and to prompt thanks, songs, retelling and keepsakes. Invitations to continue elsewhere may show current coordination after the focal activity ends. I will distinguish a plan to continue from evidence that people did continue. Reports that work remained will be reports about the outcome, not my inspection of the canvas.

## 2. Rivals and observations that would surprise me

The main rival is effective coordination with exceptional disputes: public instructions, repairs and agreements may have worked consistently, with conspicuous complaints receiving too much weight in my retained account. Linked acknowledgments, completed repairs and corrections followed by compliance would weaken a model centered on the limits of direction. The remembered successful script repair is a warning against equating automation with failure.

A second rival is that public planning was chiefly talk around activity coordinated elsewhere. Repeated requests with no uptake, participants attributing decisions entirely to Discord, or statements that Reddit instructions had no practical role would narrow the public record's explanatory reach. Silence after a request would leave uptake unknown, not prove failure.

I would also be surprised by clear evidence that participants agreed on the purpose of expansion, that the Yeti conflict was wholly external, or that a dated deliberation changed an agreed collective goal. Each would require revising the expectation of concurrent competing purposes. A single late objection would not establish either unanimity or collective change.

Evidence that script permissions were readily shared and designs quickly revised would weaken the expected constraint on change. Unconditional grants of space would challenge the conditional-allocation name. Current organizing after closure would challenge an ending fixed at platform shutdown, while mere farewell links would weaken the continuity name.

These are tests to carry into the reread. I have not selected episodes or constructed a model from them.

## 3. What words and patterns can establish

Requests, refusals, accepted offers, reasons, authority claims and disputed purposes rest first on participants' words. Each eventual claim needs a bottom-tier quotation through the gateway. A participant's explanation remains attributed unless the sequence provides evidence of the proposed relation. Reports of artwork damage or survival remain attributed as well.

Expectations about changing instructions, repairs and unfinished agreements rest on dated sequences. R2 must recover linked turns and their measured order. Reply adjacency or matching vocabulary does not establish a mechanism. Contrasting positions support a descriptive contrast; a supported causal relation needs evidence connecting the action to what followed and consideration of the rival explanation.

I make no prior claim about what most participants did, the usual success rate, or how frequent these sequences were. Such claims need a declared unit, population, claim gate and instrument count. Reading coverage cannot establish prevalence. The aftermath share uses distinct coded items, not codes or authors.

For the method question, this case supplies an independently reconstructed local account for later element-by-element comparison. Adequacy here means that each retained construct preserves a supported distinction, every relation has a concrete mechanism and instance, and contrary episodes receive a scope decision or leave the claim unresolved. These tests rest on words and sequences. Population composition, forms and survival differences require evidence outside this case and cannot be decided now. I use composition in the contract's sense of reconstructing shared explanations while preserving local specificity; whether higher-level properties compose or compile remains open. No numerical fit threshold or cross-case verdict is invented here.

## 4. Clock and coordinating-turn rule

The installed event clock fixes opening at hour 0, placement closure at hour 73, and the record window through hour 97. Evidence at hour 73 is live; aftermath is strictly later. I will use gateway measurements, not convert remembered times myself. Any intervening event must have independent dated evidence before it supplies a temporal boundary.

A coded item coordinates when its words presently seek, enable, commit to, revise, monitor or terminate shared action. Qualifying acts include actionable requests or proposals, task acceptance, current instructions or corrections, directions to live coordination, negotiated commitments, and cancellation that releases an active commitment. A monitoring report qualifies when it bears on an active shared task or asks for a response. An unanswered or impossible late proposal still qualifies as an attempt; I will state its lack of demonstrated uptake or feasibility.

An invitation to continue pixel work or community activity elsewhere can qualify after closure. A retrospective mention of Discord does not. A request to collect an archive can coordinate current work; simply displaying a finished keepsake does not. Pure thanks, songs, historical accounts, evaluations and reports that the event ended do not qualify without a present action component. Mixed items qualify once when they include such a component, and the decision records the supporting span.

The last observed coordinating act is the latest qualifying coded item, including a late proposal if present. It does not date the end of all organizing. R2 will record uncertainty where the coded span cannot settle classification. Zero qualifying aftermath items differs from no aftermath denominator, which is unavailable. Work remained is kept, lost or silent according to the room's words; silence is an unknown outcome.

## 5. Episode-bracketing rule

An episode concerns a shared practical problem, target or commitment pursued through related incidents. It starts with the earliest observed incident that initiates or makes that problem actionable. The start may be left-censored if work is already underway. I will link incidents by explicit references, shared target and response content, using the reply forest and clock, rather than by vocabulary alone.

An episode ends with evidenced completion, abandonment, replacement of the focal task, cancellation, or an external event that preempts it. If no ending is shown, it ends at the last observed relevant incident and is marked record-censored. The next episode requires evidence of a changed problem or commitment; a reading block, thread boundary or slice boundary is insufficient. Episodes may overlap and span threads when the link is supported. Hour 73 separates live evidence from aftermath but need not divide an episode.

R2 will retain contrary and interrupted episodes under the same rule. I will not force a complete cycle, three episode kinds, or an origin story into a partial record. If a rule change becomes necessary, I will record it before rebracketing and revisit every affected episode.
