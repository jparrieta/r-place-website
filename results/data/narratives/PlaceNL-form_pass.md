## What the pass cannot reach

The code excerpts do not expose every response, missing body, edit time, private message or Discord decision. String matching would identify words while missing stance and sequence. Topic groupings can hide whether a passage requests, refuses, promises or performs thanks. These sweeps restore that distinction only where words and context permit it. A missing observed answer remains unknown. The single unexplained grievance `c0239` cannot ground an account of its cause.
