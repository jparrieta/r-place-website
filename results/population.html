The population model

How 705 community records become a shared explanation, and where communities differ from it.

What the population is

The population is every community that organized for the event in writing: 705 subreddits, kept from the 2,796 that the atlas credits with an artwork because their members wrote about the event while the canvas was open. Their 2,130,302 posts and comments run from the week before the event to the day after it. Seventy-eight of them, like r/PlaceNL, were founded for the event.
