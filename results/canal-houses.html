The canal houses and the repair claim

With a few hours left on the canvas, members of r/PlaceNL wanted to change the Dutch flag. Two records show what they agreed to build and what got in their way.

How the coder read the two records

The coder labelled the post planning blue canal houses and a windmill on the flag and grouped it with other proposals for Dutch words, pictures and borders. It labelled the comment asking who will turn off the defense script erasing the houses and grouped it with accounts of making, fixing and arguing over scripts.

One label records the design members wanted. The other records the work it took to change what their script protected. Read the plan code · Read the script code.

Across 701 posts and comments, the coder produced 246 codes and 21 themes. Its case account and timeline place the houses alongside the community’s other work.

What the case changed in the model

The aggregator built a model from the coded cases and sent it back to the community coders. When the Dutch coder compared the model with the flag episode, it identified a missing condition. Members could agree to a redesign while their script still enforced the old one. The aggregator accepted this as a correction to its claim about settling a design.

The related claim about repair changed after objections from r/OnePiece and r/leagueoflegends. Keepers could erase a planned change until they learned of it. The Dutch records showed how a script could produce that result. Read the two rulings · See the revised population model.

How r/PlaceNL came to sit in form B

The houses tell us about repair. To place r/PlaceNL on the tree, the analysis used its full profile of 22 selected changes and 246 coded concepts. It gave changes two thirds of the weight and codes one third. The Dutch community fell among the 141 in form B, where chat, scripts and calls to raid featured in different combinations.
