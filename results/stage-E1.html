    The audit and reproducible calculations

    audit-E1.md reports checks, limits and the account of methods. audit-E1/ holds scripts and outputs for replay. claims-E1.md checks whether each claim received the kind of evidence it needs: source words or a pattern across cases. probes-E1.md reports recall checks and separates forgotten information from questions the record could not answer.
