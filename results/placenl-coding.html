r/PlaceNL: community coding

Community
r/PlaceNL, founded for the event to coordinate the Dutch artworks
Record
701 posts and comments in 101 threads; nothing in the week before
Coded by
one community coder and two fleet coders, gpt-5.6-sol, 7 September 2026
Result
246 codes on 228 items, 21 themes, a case account and a timeline

The record is silent until 27.67 hours after the canvas opened, so the account cannot say how the community began. Its first visible act is a declaration of official coordination. Codes, themes and the account below are the community coder's; the English in brackets is ours.

The plan and its venue

The coder codes the declaration, then a running conflict between the posted plan and a faster plan on Discord.

Building and erasing

Late on the third day, members plan new Dutch pictures on the flag. The community's own defense script, built to repair damage, erases them. The coder records both, without deciding which caused what.

From the account: These are contrasting practices in the same period, not evidence that the community collectively changed from conquest to restraint. The coder keeps what members did apart from what it could prove set it off.
