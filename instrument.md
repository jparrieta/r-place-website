# How the instrument works

The instrument turns the shared r/place record into accounts that later studies can test and revise. It is one part of the repository's [shared data and tools](tools.html).

AI readers develop codes and themes from participants’ words. An AI aggregator compares those accounts and develops a shared model under researcher supervision. Saved case agents then compare it with their community records. These returns are not interviews with the original participants. Adaptations support a taxonomy of organizational forms.

The analyst owns the interpretation. Checks establish what was read, written and preserved; they do not establish that an explanation is true.
