The community coder's documents

Every document a community went through, from its brief to its local model, reproduced unchanged. How community coding works explains them in order.

The coder instrument

Version 32 coded 603 communities and version 30 coded 102, r/PlaceNL among them. Version 32 is in the repository; version 30 comes from r/PlaceNL's checkpoint. In the four communities checked, the run contract differs from its template only in ten lines: the community's name and the paths to its record, brief, tools and outputs.

The handler

  • The scheduler's contract: what it may read and write, the cycle for one community, the checks, failures, the record, and what is ours to decide.
  • The coding scheduler: how many communities run at once, and the order of the queue.
  • The case-lead prompt: how a community coder is launched and calibrated.
  • Checkpoints: the saved session of each community coder.
  • The brief coordinator and the brief writer's prompt: how the briefs were queued and written.
  • Repair-queue rules: how failed work is restarted.
  • Verified archives: how a finished community is archived and restored.
