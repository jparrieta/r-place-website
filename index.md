# An Organizational Fruit Fly

r/place as a model organism for cumulative grounded theory.

Over 72 hours in 2017, more than a million people built and defended pictures on a shared canvas. The pixels show what changed. In their discussions, people proposed designs, settled borders and called for help.
We brought these records together for 705 communities. Like biologists returning to the fruit fly, researchers can return to the same cases, compare explanations and test what earlier studies missed.

## How the study works

The study has three parts, run in order.

1. **[Collection](how-collection-works.html)** Every pixel change, joined with the posts and comments of the communities behind the artworks. A census decides which 705 communities count as cases.
2. **[Community coding](how-coding-works.html)** One AI agent reads each community's full record and codes how its members organized. It sees no other community.
3. **[Aggregation](how-it-works.html)** A second agent composes the 705 coded cases into one population model. It returns the model to every community coder, revises it, and groups the communities into candidate organizational forms.

## What we found

The run followed one community closely and all 705 together. [From form to post](results/form-to-post.html) traces one of the six organizational forms back to the posts behind it.

1. **[One community: r/PlaceNL](results/placenl.html)** Founded for the event to coordinate the Dutch artworks. Its coders turned 701 posts and comments into 246 codes and 21 themes. At the return its coder reported canal houses erased by the community's own defense script; corrections from other communities revised the model's account of repair, and r/PlaceNL's line became a specification of it.
2. **[The population model](results/population.html)** Each community coded on its own, then composed into eight aggregate dimensions and one model. Communities that asked for similar changes to the model form six candidate organizational forms.
