# An Organizational Fruit Fly

r/place as a model organism for cumulative grounded theory.

Biology accumulates knowledge around the fruit fly. Organization science has no common organism to study. From 31 March to 3 April 2017, over a million Reddit users spent 72 hours making and defending pictures on a shared canvas, one pixel at a time. We propose this event as a model organism for how organizations emerge and evolve.

Here you find the frozen record of the event, the instrument we used to read it, and what it found in 705 communities.

## How the study works

The study has three parts, run in order. Each takes the one before it as input and works under its own written instructions.

1. **[Collection](how-collection-works.html)** Every pixel change, joined with the posts and comments of the communities behind the artworks. A census decides which 705 communities count as cases.
2. **[Community coding](how-coding-works.html)** One AI agent reads each community's full record and codes how its members organized. It sees no other community.
3. **[Aggregation](how-it-works.html)** A second agent composes the 705 coded cases into one population model. It returns the model to every community coder, revises it, and groups the communities into candidate organizational forms.

Researchers judge what the model is worth. [How the instrument works](instrument.html)
