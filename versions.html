Versions of the instrument

How the contracts came to their current versions, and how the population model changed during the run. All dates are 2026. Time and costs gives how long each stage took.

Contract versions

Written contracts specify what each agent reads, produces and may do. We developed them through repeated runs on the same material and interviews with the agents, and revised a contract when the agents' work departed from the grounded-theory method.

Community coders, v1 to v32. Only v30 (102 communities) and v32 (603) coded the final data.

The main revisions:

  • C3 and C4. Two arrows take a mechanism from conversation analysis. Two elements and an arrow enter from r/place, the field, as tentative.
  • C5. Three elements are cut for want of a grounded arrow; one of them is restored.
  • D3, from the community coders' returns. The starter's role is split from joining, which the model now treats as the answer to a call. Repair is revised: it restores the form the keepers know, so a planned change is erased as damage until the keepers learn of it. r/PlaceNL is among the communities that showed this (the example). The elements and arrow from r/place become standing, and fifteen candidates enter. We froze the model as 46384686.
  • D4. Two changes made after the freeze were reverted to proposals, restoring 46384686.
